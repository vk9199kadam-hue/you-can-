Ask any question from your Maharashtra Board syllabus

Can you explain Gauss's Law with a simple example? I'm confused about when to use it vs Coulomb's Law.
AI
AI Tutor — Physics

Gauss's Law states that the total electric flux through any closed surface equals the enclosed charge divided by permittivity. Use it when charge distribution has symmetry (spherical, cylindrical, or planar). Use Coulomb's Law for point charges without symmetry.
